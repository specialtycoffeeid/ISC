Pre-shipment cupping

Arrival cupping

Moisture & density QC

Small-batch splitting

Export docs & freight

Questions

What buyers ask before their first order

What is green coffee?

Green coffee is the raw, unroasted seed of the coffee cherry after processing and drying. It is the form coffee is traded and shipped in, because it stays stable for months, where roasted coffee is best within weeks. Everything we sell is green — you or your roaster apply the roast.

What does an SCA score of 85+ mean?

The Specialty Coffee Association cupping form scores a coffee out of 100 across attributes including aroma, flavour, acidity, body and balance. A score of 80 or above is classed as specialty. Our floor is 85, which sits in the upper part of that band. Scores are assigned by cupping a prepared sample, not estimated from the coffee's origin or appearance.

What is the minimum order?

One kilogram. Small orders are split from the same lot that fills the wholesale sacks, so a home roaster buying 1 kg and a roastery buying 500 kg receive coffee from the same lot number and crop year.

Do you ship samples internationally?

Yes. Sample sets ship from Jakarta and are drawn from the lot currently on offer. Tell us the origins and profiles you are interested in when you request pricing and we will match the set to what you actually roast.

How do you verify traceability?

Each lot carries a lot number that ties together the producer or producer group, the mill, the crop year, the process, the altitude band and the export documents. The cupping scores from before shipment and on arrival are filed against the same number, and the retained samples stay on the desk.

What is the difference between washed and wet-hulled coffee?

In the washed process the fruit is removed and the beans are fermented and washed before drying to a stable moisture level, which tends to produce a cleaner, more acidic cup. In wet-hulling — common in Sumatra and known locally as giling basah — the parchment is removed at a much higher moisture content and drying finishes afterwards, which typically gives lower acidity, heavier body and the earthy, herbal character associated with Indonesian coffee.

How should green coffee be stored?

Keep it cool, dark, dry and away from strong smells, in a breathable or barrier bag depending on how long you are holding it. Green coffee at a stable moisture content around 10–12% holds its character for months; heat and humidity swings are what age it fastest. Small orders ship vacuum-sealed for this reason.
